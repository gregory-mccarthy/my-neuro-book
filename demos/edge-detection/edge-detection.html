What is idealised here. The retina is a 128 × 128 grid with one cell of each type at every point, so neighboring receptive fields overlap heavily, as real ones do; real mosaics differ in density by cell type and by eccentricity, and here every receptive field is the same size. Center and surround are Gaussians. The surround is drawn as a horizontal-cell pool that acts instantly; real surrounds lag the center by tens of milliseconds and are reshaped by amacrine cells before the ganglion cells. There is no time course at all: each demonstration shows the steady response to a steady pattern. Photoreceptors report contrast, (light − adapted level) ÷ adapted level, and adapt instantly to the mean light of the whole scene; real adaptation is partly local and takes time. Unticking adaptation freezes the adapted level at its full-light value. The ganglion cells inherit the bipolar signal through a straight line clipped at 0 and 100 Hz; the 22 Hz maintained rate and the gain of 150 Hz per unit of bipolar response are schematic, and real ON and OFF cells are not exact mirror images. Image edges are extended by repeating the border, so the frame itself is not an edge. The stimulus panel encodes luminance for a display with gamma 2.2; brightness illusions in that panel depend on your screen. The house and the letters are drawn, not photographed. An image you load is processed only in your browser and is never sent anywhere.
