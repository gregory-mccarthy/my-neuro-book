      Climbing-fiber background

      Off is a deliberately unbalanced demonstration: local LTP continues at its usual rate but nothing balances it outside error-driven CF events, so predictions drift upward until overprediction drives enough CF events to cancel LTP.

      The local LTP rate constant is calibrated to this baseline (LTP = η · p₀ · eligibility), so any of these settings is balanced on average when the error is zero.

      Timing of the teaching signal

      With late feedback, error-modulated CF events arrive when no synapse is eligible, so they cause no LTD and no learning. You can also press Trigger CF during and after the eligibility glow.

      What the model computes

      GC features are fixed products of MF inputs, for example GC6 = elbow position × biceps tension. PC prediction for cell i is Σj wij·GCj, using the weights at the moment of prediction. The prediction is saved and never recomputed.

      Eligibility of synapse GCj→PCi: e = GCj·exp(−t/300 ms), treated as expired once the factor falls below 0.15 (≈570 ms).

      Plasticity opportunity every 100 ms of model time: local LTP adds η·p₀·e at every eligible synapse; each PC independently draws a CF event with probability p. A CF event at PCi subtracts η·e from that PC's eligible synapses only. Weights are kept in [0, 1].

      CF probability is the baseline p₀ = 0.20, except in the feedback-modulated window (400 ms after feedback arrives), where pi = clip(p₀ − k·ri, 0, 1) with k = 2.5 and ri = actual − saved prediction. Overprediction (r < 0) means more CF events; underprediction (r > 0) means fewer, so LTP wins. When r = 0, LTP and CF-driven LTD balance on average. η = 0.05.

      What is not claimed

      • Granule cells do not literally compute these products; they stand in for a rich recoding of mixed inputs.
      • Each PC is a separate cell whose activity is read as one prediction coordinate. Real Purkinje cells are inhibitory at their output; this model does not simulate the cerebellar nuclei.
      • The IO register is a functional teaching abstraction. Inferior-olive neurons do not hold a numerical outcome or perform this subtraction.
      • Labels such as “background” and “feedback-modulated” describe how the simulator generated an event. A real Purkinje cell receives no such label, and not every complex spike induces LTD.
      • Model milliseconds are schematic. The display is slowed for viewing; the seconds on screen are not biological delays.
