Cerebellar learning: adapting the vestibulo-ocular reflex (VOR)

A trial-by-trial rate model of the flocculus, vestibular nuclei and inferior olive. Learning happens at two sites: quickly in the flocculus (Ito's hypothesis) and slowly in the vestibular nuclei under Purkinje cell guidance (Miles and Lisberger's hypothesis). It is schematic: rates and time constants are in physiological ranges but are not fitted to any data set.
