From a distributed decision to a reach

A decision population settles into one of two attractors. A few weighted projections of its state reach a non-directional commitment pool and a motor population, whose fixed linear muscle readout drives a three-joint arm. Nothing in the running model issues a “go left” command or computes joint torques outside the population.

What the model assumes, and what it does not show

Decision population. 160 rate units with one rank-one choice structure plus random full-rank connectivity. Units come in mirror pairs with opposite choice loadings (the same symmetry as two-pool decision models), so there is no built-in left or right bias; the display order is shuffled. Recurrent gain rises with urgency, so the network starts as a leaky integrator and becomes bistable. Noise is scaled by √dt.

The fork is measured, not assumed. The nominal gain g crosses 1 at 1.2 s, but the random connectivity also contributes to the effective gain, so the undecided state can lose stability later (about 1.6 s in the default network; it varies between networks). The state-space panel draws the fork found by settling the noise-free network at each urgency level.

Axes are fitted, not assumed. Before the first trial the network runs 60 calibration trials. The choice, urgency and context directions are estimated from those trials. The “rotated-away” readout is orthogonal to every direction along which left and right trials differ on average at any time, so a 90° rotation removes linearly decodable choice information rather than merely scaling it down.

Channel. Each transmitted dimension is a fixed weight vector applied to the rates, plus noise. The motor side filters it with a 50 ms synaptic time constant. The choice signal reaches the motor population’s target inputs through an additional 150 ms filter; the size of the serial-control effect depends on that assumed time constant.

Commitment is still a bound. Some mechanism has to decide when to move. Here it is a recurrent pool that sees |c₁| and urgency but not the sign of the choice. It removes the directional command, not the threshold.

Motor population. Units have cosine tuning to limb-segment and joint angles (a quarter have Gaussian posture fields), multiplied by mixed tuning to the vector errors to both targets and to the start pad, context and wrist angle. Engagement and urgency enter additively and through unit-specific gains. Six muscle activations are a fixed linear readout, rectified. There is no gate: preparation is output-null only because the readout learned to ignore it.

The readout was learned from a teacher. During the build step, the readout is fitted by ridge regression to a Jacobian-transpose controller with a null-space posture term, first with the teacher driving the arm, then with the population increasingly driving it. The teacher is never called while trials run. This shows a population can implement state-dependent control; it says nothing about how that mapping is learned.

The choice acts as input gain. The communicated choice signal multiplies the input from each visible target (a biased-competition assumption). With a weak or absent choice signal both targets are weighted equally, which is why the hand heads toward the midpoint when the channel is rotated away.

Known limitations. Endpoint error is typically 3–15 px on reaches of about 90 px, worse than the teacher’s 1–2 px. Reaches are slow, partly because urgency raises co-contraction. Joint dynamics have no inertial coupling. The attractor has little spare gain: silencing 8 of the most selective units leaves choices and reaches intact but delays the fork, and silencing 16–24 weakens or abolishes bistability. Muscles are agonist–antagonist pairs, not a musculoskeletal model.

What would count against the claims. A posture comparison where motor-population correlation stays near the same-posture control (about 1.0). A 90° rotation that still yields correct reaches. Hand drift during long preparation comparable to the reach itself. Context changing the wrist angle at rank 1. A small lesion that abolishes the choice. Each of these can be checked with the controls above.
