What to notice

One gradient gives one number. Overlapping gradients give each cell a richer molecular address without naming the cell individually.

This demonstration models developmental logic, not a literal molecular reconstruction. Real neural patterning uses many interacting morphogens, transcription factors, organizers, feedback loops, and tissue mechanics.
