Integration in a Neuron: EPSPs, IPSPs, and Summation

A simplified but principled animation of dendritic integration. Graded potentials spread from each synapse, attenuate and slow with distance, sum across space and time, and can trigger an action potential if the membrane potential at the axon initial segment reaches threshold.
