What is being modeled?

Five already-patterned neural territories begin with the same simple rule: while proliferation is ON, divide once per developmental cycle; when the local stop signal arrives, differentiate and stop dividing.

The lever is timing.
One extra proliferative cycle doubles the relative output of that territory in this deliberately idealized model.

Real neurogenesis is asynchronous and is not a sequence of perfect whole-population doublings. The exponential rule is used here to make the consequence of developmental duration visible.
