A toy dentate gyrus in ten steps

Can the dentate gyrus tell Monday from Tuesday?

You park in the same garage on Monday and on Tuesday. The two mornings are almost identical, yet you need to remember them as separate events.

Stored as they are, patterns this alike would blur together: a cue for one morning would bring back the other. One route from the entorhinal cortex to CA3, the memory store, runs through the dentate gyrus.

Can the dentate gyrus make Monday and Tuesday more different?

Step 1 of 10 · The inputs

Ten inputs, each on or off

In this toy the entorhinal cortex has 10 input units, F1 to F10 (F for feature). On a given morning each one is either active (1) or silent (0).

In the brain each feature would be carried by many entorhinal neurons. Ten units keep the arithmetic visible.

Step 4 of 10 · The threshold

Inhibition sets a bar

Granule cells are held down by strong inhibition from local interneurons. A cell fires only if its total input reaches a threshold, τ (tau).

    Step 9 of 10 · The threshold’s effect

    What the threshold does

    τ splits the plane into four: both mornings, Monday only, Tuesday only, silent. One-morning cells exist only near the corner at (τ, τ).

    Raise τ with the slider at the top: fewer cells fire, more of them are one-morning cells, and output similarity tends to fall.

    Step 10 of 10 · The yardstick

    Is it really separation?

    For raw overlap: what hurts CA3 is overlap among active cells, which lets one cue reactivate another memory. For correcting: any sparser code has a lower raw cosine, even one that loses information.

    Where next

    What this toy leaves out

    • Each feature stands for a group of entorhinal neurons, not one.
    • Real granule cells receive far more than ten inputs, sampled sparsely from a much larger entorhinal population.
    • Inhibition is partly feedback, so the effective threshold rises with total input. The ≈4% button in step 9 mimics that; a fixed τ does not.
    • Synapses are fixed. Plasticity, mossy cells and adult-born granule cells are left out.
