    Model options and assumptions

    • Activity is in normalised units (0–1), not Hz. All parameters are teaching choices, not fits to data.
    • Rates shown for D1/D2, GPe, STN, GPi, SNr and thalamus are the putamen-centred motor loop. Caudate is anatomical context only.
    • Channels A and B are populations within one loop. Their separate wiring and the 40% spread of STN influence to the other channel are model assumptions.
    • Dopamine changes SPN responsiveness to cortical input (D1 up, D2 down). The Huntington's factor scales the iSPN population output to GPe; it is separate from dopamine.
    • Guided steps hold downstream populations fixed while one link acts, so the order of steps is explanatory, not exact biological timing. Continuous view runs everything together.
    • The action-expression readout is a smoothed ramp of motor-cortex activity: 0 below 0.68, 1 above 0.95 (40 ms smoothing). Latency is reported when it crosses 0.2.
