UNIT-WIDE DECISIONS

Navigation remains the biological starting point of the explanation. Comparative
data support an old spatial-relational system; they do not establish the precise
chronological order in which every relational function evolved. Living fish are
not unchanged ancestors. Preserve the author's interpretation, identifying it
as an interpretation where necessary rather than flattening it into neutrality.

Keep regional ancestry, subfield correspondence, cell-type similarity, circuit
organization, and behavioral function distinct. A combination of developmental
position, expression, cellular evidence, and connectivity supports homology;
one protein or one maze result cannot establish every level of correspondence.

Keep map structure, current value, and policy distinguishable without assigning
each to an isolated brain region. Functional influence is not evidence of a
direct anatomical projection. The overview gives the circuit scaffold; detailed
anatomical elaboration should serve the experiment being explained.

Repeated cache, fish, exploration, sequence, and amnesia examples are justified
when they answer a new question. Minimize repeated manifestos about archives,
inner navigators, spatial-first evolution, and allostatic investment. Let the
mechanism or experiment carry the later recurrence.

DRAWING THE MAP — CHANGES AND CONSEQUENTIAL JUDGMENTS

Preserved the main progression from the moving animal through population codes,
path integration, development, transitions, theta, choice points, and ripples.
Reduced the repeated comparative-anatomy/lesion argument and the long concluding
evolutionary manifesto. Navigation remains the biological explanatory starting
point; it is no longer presented as proof that every nonspatial capacity arose

Retained firing-rate-map construction and added the distinction between
information recoverable by an investigator's decoder and a demonstrated neural
readout. Corrected the Frank route example from a T-maze to a W-track. Separated
route-dependent firing, rate remapping, and global remapping; coordinated grid
realignment is not equated with arbitrary movement of individual place fields.
Specified allocentric direction for boundary-vector tuning. Replaced the

Peng 2025 now identifies the outward lever search, press, and return home for
food, and separates loss of a stable room-centered lattice from retained
movement information. Reference-frame reanchoring and directional drift are
related to behavior without claiming grid-only causal control. Hardcastle's
boundary-related correction is described as neural drift/correction; border-cell
input is a proposed mechanism supported by modeling, not a selectively proven
necessary pathway. Removed the implication that wall contact is always tactile.

Development separates internal coherence from external anchoring. Pre-eye-opening
coherence is not experience-free development. Place firing before mature grid
periodicity excludes a prerequisite for that mature pattern, not all entorhinal
contributions. Developmental onset does not establish a head-direction -> place
-> grid causal chain and is not an evolutionary sequence.

The successor representation is explicitly expected discounted occupancy under
a specified policy. It does not contain every possible route or immediately
update all cached predictions when a barrier changes. Value, available
transitions, and policy remain distinguishable. The transition section now
hands event-dependent updating to Diary.

Expanded the mechanism account by separating single-cell phase precession from
within-cycle population order. Added Foster & Wilson 2007's coordination result
and explained the converging CA3/Schaffer and direct entorhinal layer III inputs
to CA1. Guardamagna's terminal-inhibition result supports a dissociation of
precession and sequences, not a unique or complete generator. Compression offers
possibilities for comparison and plasticity without proving one learning rule.

Choice-point decoding and Yu 2026's Honeycomb goal bias are distinguished from
causal proof of planning or subjective imagination. Yu's within-session increase
in goal modulation occurred in trained animals without a parallel reliable
behavioral improvement; it is not described as simple learning of the task.

Added Lee & Wilson 2002 to ground the sleep sequence account. Ripple detection,
decoded order, and replay of a particular experience are distinguished. Separated
post-training rest/sleep interruption from awake W-track interruption, including
the latter's outbound/inbound dissociation. Interventions affect accompanying
discharge as well as the oscillation; they do not establish every ripple's
function or the necessity of one decoded trajectory. The new closing bridge
explains route/context distinctions and sequence order as ingredients for event
memory, leaving need-dependent retrieval and human recollection to Diary.

Completed the final individual pass around the question: How do particular

Exploration and the controller:
  Latent learning separates acquisition from rewarded expression; the rats were
  not described as unmotivated and their behavior does not uniquely identify a
  map code. Exploration can prepare future options without being universally
  beneficial or confined to bodily sufficiency. Map structure, affordances,
  event evidence, present value, and policy are distinguished as interacting
  contributions, not sealed anatomical modules. Goals can affect sampling and
  representations. Removed the claim that a map without a diary necessarily
  predicts a stationary world: maps can already encode change and uncertainty.

Animal events and mechanisms:
  Scrub-jay recovery depends on food/place/retention interval and learned
  perishability. The rat odor-location task concerns event order, not the same
  temporal variable. Behavioral conjunctions do not demonstrate autonoesis.
  Drawing's population distinctions, remapping, theta sequences, and ripple
  activity are applied to overlapping events without reteaching spatial coding.
  New use of existing chettih2024barcoding connects familiar locations to distinct
  cache-event patterns. The neural recurrence remains a recording result, not
  proof of causal retrieval, complete stored content, or remote destination choice.

Kennedy–Shapiro:
  Separated the 2004 postoperative lesion evidence from the 2009 recordings.
  Three learned goal objects change locations; bodily state specifies the
  appropriate object–outcome relation. Retained avoidance of the unrewarded box
  and the tested motivational/discrimination controls without declaring all
  perception and motivation normal. Neurotoxic hippocampal damage and fornix
  interruption are distinct interventions. A lasting postoperative impairment
  does not isolate retrieval from every retention/interference contribution.
  Both deprivation conditions were used during training: the experiment did not
  demonstrate learning while sated followed by first use under need. This is
  conditional memory-guided choice, not simply a match between encoding and
  retrieval state, and not a direct test of one unique training episode.
  Recording modulation and prospective target coding are task-dependent
  associations, not another causal manipulation or direct measure of bodily need.

Wearing and H.M. anatomy:
  Wearing's report includes bilateral hippocampal/amygdalar abnormalities,
  asymmetric temporal cortical damage, mammillary/basal-forebrain involvement,
  and additional milder abnormalities. His waking conviction is distinguished
  from the general episodic impairment. The diary is behavior/belief evidence,
  not a transparent report of isolated hippocampal loss or a proven mechanism
  of temporal continuity. Preserved music is not explained by a single label.
  H.M.'s broad bilateral medial-temporal resection is described with posterior
  hippocampal tissue and substantial posterior parahippocampal sparing. Entorhinal
  damage compromised communication; residual tissue is not declared wholly
  nonfunctional. Additional pathology is not all assigned to the 1953 operation.

H.M., remote memory, and the author's critical position:
  The original report's broad remote-sparing description is separated from
  claims about intact detailed episodes. The 2005 primary paper supports severe
  remote autobiographical impairment with relatively preserved knowledge, but
  also documents a detailed flight at age fifteen and an undated wedding memory.
  These exceptions are now included. The result is not complete episodic absence.
  Late testing cannot establish every immediate postoperative capacity. Added
  Kirwan et al. 2008 because it directly tests detailed remote recollection in
  other patients and raises the later-pathology alternative for H.M.
  This qualification does not supply missing early evidence of intact episodic
  memory. The critique remains explicit: H.M. cannot be the uncomplicated proof
  that richly detailed episodes survive independently of hippocampal operations.
  Removed unsupported claims about all textbooks, generations of students, or
  the motives of an entire field. No systematic textbook-reception audit was
  performed. Consolidation theory drew on other patients and animal experiments
  as well as H.M.; his case was not its sole logical foundation. Corkin's book
  remains in the cumulative bibliography, but its unverified specific historical
  characterization is no longer used as a substitute for primary testing.

Consolidation theories:
  The 1995 CLS paper explicitly treats graded retrograde amnesia as central and
  extends its reinstatement account to episodic task information, conditional on
  the context carried by reinstatement. It does not depict cortex as initially
  empty or promise automatic unchanged survival of every episode. The critique
  now targets the further inference from successful cortical performance to
  preservation of rich episodic reconstruction. Complementary learning rates and
  systems reorganization do not entail that conclusion or establish evolutionary
  history. Added Kumaran et al. 2016 to identify later schema-sensitive and broader
  formulations rather than assigning every strong claim to all CLS variants.
  Multiple Trace Theory and transformation are now distinguished: additional
  distributed traces versus coexistence of context-rich and schematic forms.
  Both permit reorganization; transformation need not destroy the detailed form.
  Added a comparison table covering theory commitments and discriminating tests.
  H.M.'s result is congenial to the event/knowledge distinction, not unique proof
  of MTT or a final resolution of hippocampal necessity for all remote episodes.

Reactivation and engrams:
  Expanded the Kitamura study to explain activity-dependent tagging, changing
  natural cue recruitment, inhibition, and direct activation separately. Cortical
  ensembles participate early and mature; hippocampal ensemble output contributes
  to that process. Later artificial dentate activation can evoke freezing despite
  loss of preferential natural context recruitment. This is evidence against
  equating natural inaccessibility with trace destruction, not proof of ordinary
  retrieval or a complete human-like episode. Different assays/cohorts/days remain
  distinct. The study supports changing circuit dependence in the fear task;
  it is not recruited as proof or disproof of lifelong human episodic dependence.

Construction and development:
  Addis's construction and elaboration phases, common recruitment, and differences
  are separated; regional BOLD overlap does not identify identical neurons or
  one identical computation. Hassabis's five-patient study includes a relatively
  spared participant. Maguire 2010 concerns Jon; it is not a population proof of
  universal developmental compensation. The 1997 developmental cases demonstrate
  substantial semantic learning amid severe episodic impairment, not absence of
  all hippocampal function. Residual tissue, support, and alternative learning
  routes remain possible contributors rather than measured mechanisms. Timing
  within development remains distinct from causal dependence and evolution.

Unit closure:

  spatial-first evolutionary chronology. Living fish are not unchanged ancestors.
